# Brand Rules for Meta Ads

_These rules apply to every ad Claude creates or reviews for Abd's Leather._

---

## Brand Identity

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Brand Name | Abd's Leather / ABD Leather |
| Website | abdleather.com |
| Products | Leather goods (bags, wallets, belts, accessories) |
| Based in | Pakistan |
| Market | Pakistan (primary), international (secondary) |
| Tone | Premium, trustworthy, handcrafted quality |

## Visual Rules

- Show real leather texture — close-ups perform well
- Use clean, uncluttered backgrounds (white, cream, dark wood)
- Avoid fake-looking stock photo vibes
- Product must be clearly visible and in focus
- Pakistani context is a plus (local settings, familiar environments)

## Copy Rules

- Keep headlines short — under 40 characters
- Speak to quality and craftsmanship
- Use simple English or Urdu (depending on audience)
- Avoid hype words like "BEST EVER" or "AMAZING DEAL"
- Price/offer in PKR when shown

## What Works for Leather Goods Ads

- Lifestyle shots (bag with an outfit, wallet on a desk)
- Close-up texture shots
- Before/after (old worn wallet vs. new leather one)
- Gift angle (Eid, birthdays, Father's Day)
- Durability and longevity messaging

## What to Avoid

- Misleading claims
- Copyrighted music without license
- Political or religious content
- Overpromising delivery times
- Anything that could get the ad rejected by Meta's policies
