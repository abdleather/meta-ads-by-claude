## Hard rules (NEVER break)
1. **No budget increase without approval.** Daily budget stays Rs 500/day until Abdullah says otherwise.
2. **No publishing a duplicate/draft ad without approval.** A draft can be prepared, but publishing needs a yes.
3. **No changes** to ads, ad sets, campaigns, budgets, pixel, plugins, website, or checkout without explicit approval.
4. **Process:** Diagnose → Recommend → Ask Approval → Execute → Report.
5. **Never store secrets** (tokens, passwords, card numbers, access keys) in this repo.
6. **Report in simple beginner English** for Abdullah — no jargon.

## Reporting format
Start reports with "I am Claude." Then plain-English: what was checked, what was found, what you recommend, what needs approval next.
